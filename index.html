Three kitchens.
One plate.

Savory Food Fusions started with a simple idea: the best flavors don't stay in their lane. Mexican quesabirria and street tacos, Asian crispy chicken, Caribbean jerk chicken, Dominican guisado and rice, a Mediterranean gyro, and Tuscan pasta all live on the same board — because if it tastes right, it goes on the menu.

Every plate is built fresh to order and posted straight from the pass — no stock photos, just what's actually coming out of the kitchen.
